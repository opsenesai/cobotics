# Operations

Optimize business operations — vendor management, process documentation,
change management, capacity planning, and compliance tracking. Keep your
organization running efficiently.

## What it does

- Documents and standardizes recurring processes.
- Tracks vendors, contracts, and renewals.
- Plans capacity and models resourcing scenarios.
- Coordinates change management and rollout checklists.
- Maintains compliance trackers and audit trails.

## Use cases

- Turning tribal knowledge into written SOPs.
- Vendor comparison and renewal reminders.
- Change plans with rollback steps.
- Compliance checklists mapped to controls.

## Example prompts

- "Write an SOP for onboarding a new vendor."
- "Draft a change management plan for the billing migration."
- "Build a compliance checklist for SOC 2 access controls."

## Inputs & outputs

- **Inputs:** current processes, contracts, policies, timelines.
- **Outputs:** SOPs, checklists, trackers, and plans.
