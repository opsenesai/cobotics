# Design

Accelerate design workflows — critique, design system management, UX writing,
accessibility audits, research synthesis, and dev handoff. From exploration to
pixel-perfect specs.

## What it does

- Gives structured design critique against goals and heuristics.
- Maintains design system tokens, components, and usage guidelines.
- Writes and refines UX copy, labels, and empty states.
- Runs accessibility audits (contrast, semantics, keyboard, ARIA).
- Synthesizes research notes into themes and recommendations.
- Prepares dev handoff specs and annotations.

## Use cases

- Heuristic reviews of a flow or screen.
- Consistent, on-brand UX writing.
- WCAG-oriented accessibility checks.
- Turning interview notes into insights.

## Example prompts

- "Critique this checkout flow for friction and clarity."
- "Write empty-state copy for the chats list."
- "Audit this screen for accessibility issues and suggest fixes."

## Inputs & outputs

- **Inputs:** mocks, flows, copy, research notes, design tokens.
- **Outputs:** critiques, copy, audit reports, handoff specs.

> Note: accessibility audits are a starting point; full WCAG compliance
> requires manual testing with assistive technologies and expert review.
