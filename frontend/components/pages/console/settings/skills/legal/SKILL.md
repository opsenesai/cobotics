# Legal

Speed up contract review, NDA triage, and compliance workflows for in-house
legal teams. Draft legal briefs, organize precedent research, and manage
institutional knowledge.

## What it does

- Reviews contracts and NDAs, surfacing risky or non-standard clauses.
- Triages incoming agreements against your playbook.
- Drafts briefs, memos, and clause libraries.
- Organizes precedent and institutional knowledge for reuse.

## Use cases

- First-pass NDA review with redline suggestions.
- Clause-by-clause comparison to a standard template.
- Summaries of long agreements for business stakeholders.
- Building and maintaining a searchable clause library.

## Example prompts

- "Review this NDA and flag anything outside our standard terms."
- "Summarize the liability and termination clauses in plain English."
- "Draft a mutual NDA based on our template."

## Inputs & outputs

- **Inputs:** contracts, templates, playbooks, prior agreements.
- **Outputs:** redlines, summaries, memos, clause libraries.

> Note: assistance is informational and not a substitute for professional
> legal advice.
